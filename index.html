About the Game

Chaos is the point.

Sync or Sink drops you and your friends into a single canoe on wild, unpredictable rivers. Each player controls one oar using their phone — scan the QR code to connect with the mobile controller app and start paddling.

The catch? The boat only goes where everyone paddles it. Getting eight people to row in the same direction is harder than it sounds, and that's exactly where the fun begins.

How It Works

Easy to Pick Up. Impossible to Master.

🔗

Scan & Play

Scan to connect. No extra controllers needed. Players scan a QR code with their phone to launch the app and instantly have a paddle in their hand.

🚣

Physics-Based Rowing

Every player's real movements translate to live oar physics. Tilt, swing, push — your phone gyroscope is the controller.

💬

Communication Required

The boat only goes where you all agree it goes. Coordination — and a lot of yelling — is the core gameplay loop.

🌊

Live Water Physics

Navigate raging rapids, sweeping currents, and atmospheric river environments with real-time water simulation.

👥

2–8 Players

Works with any group size. Get two friends rowing or fill an entire canoe — the chaos scales with the headcount.

⏱️

Race to the Finish

Complete courses against the clock. Every run is different depending on who's rowing and how badly.
