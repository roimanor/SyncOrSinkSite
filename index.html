About the Game

Chaos is the point.

Sync or Sink drops you and your friends into a single canoe on wild, unpredictable rivers. Each player controls one oar using nothing but their phone — no apps to install, just scan a QR code and start paddling.

The catch? The boat only goes where everyone paddles it. Getting eight people to row in the same direction is harder than it sounds, and that's exactly where the fun begins.

  • 📱
    Your Phone is Your Oar — Tilt, swing, and move to control your paddle. Zero setup.
  • 🌊
    Raging Rivers — Navigate treacherous rapids and unpredictable water physics.
  • 🎉
    Instant Party — Up to 8 players on one screen, no extra hardware required.

How It Works

Easy to Pick Up. Impossible to Master.

🔗

Scan & Play

No apps. No controllers. Players scan a QR code with their phone and instantly have a paddle in their hand.

🚣

Physics-Based Rowing

Every player's real movements translate to oar physics. Tilt, swing, push — your phone gyroscope is the controller.

💬

Communication Required

The boat only goes where you all agree it goes. Coordination (and a lot of yelling) is the core gameplay loop.

🌊

Treacherous Rivers

Navigate raging rapids, sweeping currents, and unexpected obstacles across handcrafted courses.

👥

2–8 Players

Works with any group size. Get two friends rowing or fill an entire canoe — the chaos scales with the headcount.

⏱️

Race to the Finish

Complete courses against the clock. Every run is different depending on who's rowing and how badly.
